Peeking Under the AI Hood: (Mostly) Unplugged AI Activities for Elementary

CSTA Membership Required

Login or register to access this content

Join CSTA Membership Required
Login
Young students are already encountering artificial intelligence in their daily lives—but can they truly understand how it works? Absolutely. In this workshop, participants will engage with (mostly) unplugged lessons designed for learners as early as Kindergarten. We’ll explore what AI is and where it appears, how it’s trained, how bias emerges, and how large language models like ChatGPT function.
