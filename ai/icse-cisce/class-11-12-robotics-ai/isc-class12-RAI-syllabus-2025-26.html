Understand Marking Scheme Through ICSE Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Syllabus for Class 10 2025-26

By going through the Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Syllabus for Class 10 2025-26, students can get an overall view about the internal assessment and external assessment. 

For the theory part, The subject is divided into section 1 and section 2. Total marks for section 1 is 40 marks and total marks for section 2 is 40 marks. Both the sections consist of 80 marks.
