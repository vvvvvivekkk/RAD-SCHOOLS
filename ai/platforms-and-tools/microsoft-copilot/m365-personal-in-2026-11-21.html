    The right subscription depends on whether you're purchasing for yourself, your family or need more advanced capabilities.

Those who need 1 TB of cloud storage, advanced security and productivity apps with Copilot1 can choose Microsoft 365 Personal, while Microsoft 365 Family is a good fit for households that want to share subscription benefits1 across multiple users.

  • For students who want the ultimate AI and productivity experience, Microsoft 365 Premium is an excellent choice. It combines extensive AI usage and exclusive Copilot features,1 advanced security, spacious cloud storage and productivity apps with Copilot1 built in that can help students stay organised, manage coursework, collaborate on projects and tackle academic responsibilities with confidence. Students looking for cloud storage, advanced security and productivity apps with Copilot1 can also choose Microsoft 365 Personal.
