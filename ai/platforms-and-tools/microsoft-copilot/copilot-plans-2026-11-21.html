Summary of benefits

Powerful productivity apps with Microsoft Copilot

Redefine what’s possible with Microsoft Copilot1 alongside you in Word, Excel,4 PowerPoint and OneNote. Collaborate with others in real time, work offline and get design, writing and speaking suggestions in each app.

Extensive usage limits for AI features

With Microsoft 365 Premium, the subscription owner gets extensive usage for AI features across Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps and the Microsoft Copilot app.1

Exclusive access to Copilot features

With Microsoft 365 Premium, the subscription owner gets access to advanced Copilot features that are only available to Premium and Pro subscribers.1 See here for more information on what exclusive features are currently available.

2 TB of secure cloud storage

Share 2 TB (2000 GB) of secure storage with up to 5 people for their photos, files, videos, emails and more. Each person can easily manage their own storage at any time, on any device.

Personal data and device protection

Protect your phones and computers with Microsoft Defender.5 Continuous scanning6 checks for viruses, malicious apps and ransomware. If risks are found, Defender will notify you and provide easy-to-follow steps to help keep your devices secure.

Secure photo and file storage

Each person can securely save their photos and files in their own personal OneDrive with built-in ransomware detection and file recovery.7 Access files across devices and protect what’s most important in individual Personal Vault folders secured with two-step identity verification.

Create images with AI

Create eye-catching images using only your words with Copilot and the power of generative AI.1

Ad-free, secure email with Copilot

Everyone can easily manage their own email accounts and calendars in Outlook with built-in security tools8 that keep their information protected. Subscription owners can use Copilot to draft emails and summarise longer threads.1

Unlock AI-powered experiences in selected Windows apps

Boost your creativity with the help of AI.1 Easily enhance your photos with advanced AI editing capabilities in the Photos app and create high-quality images using only your words in Paint. You can also use AI features in Notepad to seamlessly rewrite or summarise text for you.

All-day video calling

Join group calls and talk for up to 30 hours with up to 300 people with Microsoft Teams. Easily collaborate with others and save meeting recordings to reference later. Plus, use Copilot1 to rewrite your chat messages to adjust for tone, length, clarity and more.

Quickly create forms and polls

Use Copilot1 in Microsoft Forms to generate and rewrite polls and surveys. Easily create and refine your questions, improve response options and get a detailed analysis of results.

Easily create and manage databases for projects large or small.

With a Microsoft 365 Premium subscription, every person gets innovative new features and updates as soon as they are released, so everyone is always working with the latest and the greatest.

|

  • To manage your billing information, install or remove Microsoft 365 or Office apps from additional PCs or Macs and manage your installations of Office apps on other devices at any time, visit Install & Account management.

    Recurring billing subscriptions may be purchased from Microsoft Store, the iTunes® Store, the Mac App Store and some other retailers.     Customers with an active Microsoft 365 Personal subscription receive an additional 1 TB of OneDrive storage per subscription plus 5 GB of storage for free.

    The additional storage is applied to the OneDrive account that is linked to the Microsoft account used to create the Microsoft 365 subscription. For Microsoft 365 Family, Premium and Pro, the storage is tied to the Microsoft accounts of the household members who have been added to the subscription.

Renewals, cancellations and billing

|

  • Recurring billing is the simplest way to ensure continued service. You can set up monthly or annual recurring billing by visiting the Microsoft Account site.

    If you have a monthly subscription you can save money by converting it to an annual subscription at the Microsoft Account site.

  • They can still access and download their files by signing in to OneDrive directly using their Microsoft account. If their OneDrive account exceeds the free storage quota of 5 GB, they’ll need to save files elsewhere or buy more OneDrive storage.

  • You will be notified before you’re charged to continue your subscription for another year if you have an annual subscription.     You can cancel online by visiting the Microsoft Account site, signing in with the Microsoft account that you used to buy your subscription and then turning off recurring billing. With this approach, your subscription will automatically expire when your remaining subscription time is up. 

    To cancel immediately, contact Microsoft support and request an immediate cancellation. 

    If you purchased a recurring billing subscription from another retailer, you may have to cancel your subscription with that retailer. You can confirm this by visiting the Microsoft Account site.
