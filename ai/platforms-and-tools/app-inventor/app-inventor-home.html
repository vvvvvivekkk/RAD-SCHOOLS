Ten Years of iOS

Oct 20, 2026

In this blog post I celebrate 10 years of working on MIT App Inventor for iOS, capturing the history of this effort and how it has changed the face of the App Inventor project. Ten years ago, during a quiet long weekend in 2016, I sat at my computer and asked myself a question that every engineer loves (and occasionally regrets): "How hard could it be to create a brand new runtime for my product?" Many people had been asking about iOS support for App Inventor, and it made sense to dive in and find out the answer to that question. At the time, the goal was modest: I wanted to see if we could get a project like Hello Purr to run on an iPhone. But as I quickly discovered, the gap between a "simple idea" and a working implementation was a great technical puzzle that ended up having a lot of moving parts.

First MIT Apps for Action summer program announced for 2027

Oct 14, 2026

MIT App Inventor is pleased to announce the first MIT Apps for Action summer program for Boston Education Consulting. This two-week, in-person program will take place in summer 2027. Designed for students in grades 9–11, the program combines artificial intelligence, mobile application development, and design thinking. Guided by MIT instructional staff and teaching assistants, participants will use MIT App Inventor to design, build, and present applications addressing real-world challenges in their communities. For program details and application information, visit bostonedu.net.
