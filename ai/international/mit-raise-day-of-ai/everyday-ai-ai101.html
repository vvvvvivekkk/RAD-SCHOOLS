AI 101 for Teachers Professional Learning Series

Submitted by Kate Moore on October 27, 2023

A new professional learning series -- AI 101 for Teachers (https://code.org/ai/pl/101). Led by Code.org, ETS, ISTE, and Khan Academy. It is a five-part video series. 

  • Provides educators with strategies to protect students from risks such as misinformation, privacy loss, and algorithmic bias, making AI a responsible part of the educational experience.
  • Gives teachers a deeper understanding of the mechanics of AI, enabling tailored AI-based educational solutions to meet specific classroom needs and objectives.

The videos feature subtitles in 9 languages and are supplemented with helpful companion resources. Learn more in the summary video clip at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UCdj3MDZCGg&t=2s

In addition, the How AI Works Educational Series (https://code.org/ai/how-ai-works) provides teachers with video resources and lesson plans to give classrooms a comprehensive understanding of AI and its applications, with hands-on activities for a better understanding of each new AI concept.
