2025 Day of AI Impact Report

A year of rapid growth, both in the U.S. and beyond, greatly expanding the reach of Day of AI and RAISE’s mission. Educators in all 50 states and 175 countries now use Day of AI curricula, breaking a milestone of reaching more than 2 million students.
