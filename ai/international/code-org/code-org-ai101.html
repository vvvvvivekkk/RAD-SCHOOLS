Digital fluency, for the people teaching it

Most educators are being asked to have an opinion on AI before anyone's taught them how it works. This course closes that gap. Not with another tool tutorial, but with building digital fluency so you can understand, direct, question, and create with AI in your own practice and classroom.

Understand

What AI actually is, how it works under the hood, and where that differs from the hype.

Direct

How to put AI to work deliberately in your own planning and teaching, instead of letting it drive.

Question

How to spot bias, evaluate outputs, and know when to trust an AI tool and when not to.

Create

How to build AI into original lessons and assessments, not just bolt it onto what you already do.

What's covered

Five modules, about an hour each — roughly five hours to work through in total.

Suggested order is built for teachers new to AI, but every module stands on its own, so jump to what your classroom needs first.

Introducing AI for Educators

What AI is, and what it's already changing about your role. You'll come away able to name the real benefits and risks in a school setting, and describe why digital fluency — not just AI use — is the goal for you and your students.
