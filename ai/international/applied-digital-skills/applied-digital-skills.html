    Applied Digital Skills

    All Topics

    Teach and learn practical digital skills needed for the jobs of today and tomorrow with our video lessons that are free of charge

    • Browse All Lessons

      Explore our complete collection of Applied Digital Skills lessons.

    • Back to School

      Kick off the school year with lessons that help students express themselves, build relationships, and stay organized while learning new technology skills.

    • Celebrate Black History, Culture, & Identity

      Use these lessons to explore the importance of Black history, express your identity, and celebrate the contributions of African Americans throughout history, during Black History Month, or any time of year.

    • Celebrate Latinx History

      Use these lessons to explore the importance of Latinx history, express your identity, and celebrate the contributions made by the Latinx community during Hispanic Heritage Month, or any time of year.

    • Celebrate Earth Day

      In honor of Earth Day, learn about the environment and take action while building new digital skills with Earth Day projects.

    • College Readiness

      These lessons will help you plan and prepare for college and other education opportunities.

    • Computer Science

      These lessons will inspire and introduce your students to essential coding and computer science skills.

    • Explore Careers and Prepare for Your First Job

      These lessons will help you prepare for and find your first job while exploring various career paths.

    • Explore the World with Google Arts & Culture

      These lessons help you learn digital skills while exploring art, historic events, and iconic figures with Google Arts & Culture (http://g.co/arts)

    • Digital Art & Games

      These lessons help you express your creativity while creating fun projects using digital skills.

    • Google Workspace Training

      Learn how to use Google Workspace tools like Docs, Sheets, and Drive to help you improve productivity and collaboration in school, work and life.

    • Google Workspace for Beginners

      These lessons will teach you the necessary and basic skills for using Google Workspace Apps.

    • Online Safety and Digital Well-Being

      Video lessons engage your students in learning how to safely navigate the online world and develop skills for school, work, and life.

    • Study Skills and Learning Strategies

      These lessons help you learn effective study skills and techniques to prepare for projects, tests and exams.

    • Super Searchers

      In this course, you will learn practical information literacy skills, using the SIFT method and Google’s AI Mode. With these tools, you will be able to navigate complex digital environments thoughtfully and responsibly, and support others to do the same.
