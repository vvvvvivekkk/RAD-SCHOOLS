Teaching resources

Computing education is essential for all young people. We enable schools to teach computing and AI through high-quality classroom resources, purpose-built software tools, and professional development for teachers. All for free.

Subscribe to our newsletter

For the classroom

These resources are designed for school teachers. They contain everything you need, including lesson plans, worksheets, homework, and assessments.

Computing aimed at ages 5 to 16

In England, India, and Kenya, we create classroom resources for teaching computing, from digital skills to data literacy and AI. Tailored for local education systems and aligned with relevant curricula and standards.

The Computing Curriculum

Computing and computer science for ages 11–19

Developed with the University of Cambridge, Ada Computer Science offers online learning materials aligned with UK exam boards specifications, automatically marked questions with feedback, and teacher tools to set and review assignments.

Ada Computer Scienceopen_in_new

AI across the curriculum

Developed with Google DeepMind, Experience AI includes lessons, classroom resources, and hands-on activities for you to help students develop a foundational understanding of AI. For ages 8 to 16.

Experience AIopen_in_new

Experience CS

A free, integrated computer science curriculum for elementary and middle/primary and post-primary schools in the USA, Canada, and Ireland. Combining instructional materials, classroom resources, and professional development for teachers.

Experience CSopen_in_new

Teach with Code Classroom

Code Classroom is a free, safe tool designed to support the teaching and learning of computing and programming.

It includes a simple-to-use code editor with different programming languages, and lots of other features to support your teaching.

Try Code Classroomopen_in_new

Resources to build skills and confidence

We offer teachers in schools and other educational settings free training and support, whether you are a specialist computing teacher or want to bring elements of computing and AI into another subject.

Hello World magazine & podcast

The magazine and podcast for educators passionate about computing and AI. With insights, resources, effective practices, inspiring stories, and in-depth discussions.

Code Club

Join the global movement of computing clubs where school-age young people develop the confidence to create with digital technologies.

You can run a Code Club in your school outside of lesson time, or in a public space like a library or community centre. We have lots of free support to help you.
