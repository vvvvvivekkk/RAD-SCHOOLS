Discover UNESCO AI Competence Framework For Students

Carlos Madrid Gari
 10 December, 2024
ai-competency-framework-for-students

UNESCO has released a new publication titled AI Competency Framework for Students which aims to help educators in the integration of Artificial Intelligence. AI is changing our lives and the education system is not an exception, integrating AI learning objectives into official school curricula is vital for students around the world to engage with AI in a safe manner.

The report outlines 12 competencies across four different dimensions: Human-centred mindset; Ethics of AI; AI techniques and application and AI system design. These competencies are also part of three progression levels: undestand, apply and create.

Find more about the future of AI for students and read the full publication AI Competency Framework for Students, here.
