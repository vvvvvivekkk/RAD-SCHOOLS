What is AI4E®?

AI for Everyone (AI4E)® tries to demystify AI for the everyday men and women. We created AI4E back in 2017 to help organizations better understand AI so that they could start their own AI journey or come work together with us through our 100E initiative (https://aisingapore.org/100e). Now, with the release of version 4, we are bringing the exciting world of Generative AI to you. It would give you a good foundational understanding of AI from a layperson point of view.
