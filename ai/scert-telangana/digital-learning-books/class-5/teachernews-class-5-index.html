How To Download Telangana SCERT Books 2025

To Download Telangana SCERT (State Council of Educational Research and Training) Books, you can Follow these Steps:

  1. Visit the official website of Telangana SCERT. The website Address is scert.telangana.gov.in.
  2. On the Homepage, look for the “E-Textbooks” or “E-Books” Section. This section May vary Slightly Depending on Any website Updates, but it Generally Contains the Resources For Downloading the Books.
  3. Click on the “E-Textbooks” or “E-Books” Section. It will Redirect you to a New Page.
  4. On the New Page, you will Find a list of Different Classes or Grades. Select the Appropriate Class for which you want to Download the Books.
  5. Once you select the Class, you will see a list of Subjects For That particular class. Choose the subject for which you need the textbook.
  6. After selecting the subject, you will see a list of available textbooks for that subject. Click on the desired Textbook that you want to Download.
  7. The Selected Textbook will open in a New Tab or window as a PDF file.
  8. To download the textbook, locate the Download Button or icon on the PDF viewer’s interface. It is typically Represented by a Downward-facing Arrow or a Download Symbol.
  9. Click on the Download button, and the Textbook PDF file will be Downloaded to your computer or device.
  10. Once the Download is Complete, you can Access the Downloaded Textbook from your Computer’s Downloads folder or any other designated location.
