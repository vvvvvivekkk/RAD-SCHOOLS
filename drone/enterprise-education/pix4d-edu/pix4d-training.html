Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the difference between self-paced and instructor-led training?

    Self-paced online courses offer standardized content and economical pricing. Access the content anytime and anywhere, and contact the Training team with any questions you might have through the built-in messaging system. The interaction is not live, but you can expect a prompt answer. Instructor-led training is taught by a trainer in a live setting so that you can directly ask your questions.   • Do I need a software license to take training?

    It is not necessary that you own a software license to attend instructor-led training. If you do not own a license, you will be provided with a temporary one for the duration of the instructor-led training. You need a software license to take self-paced training, either trial or paid.

    - A computer that meets the minimum hardware requirements to process projects. You can check if your computer supports the software by processing a free dataset.
