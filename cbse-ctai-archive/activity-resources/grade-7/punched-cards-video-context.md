## Description

Suppose you have a set of cards with alphabets written on top of it. Now, after shuffling them, you want to sort them in ascending order based on alphabetical order. What will you do? You might want to search for alphabet A, then B and so on. But this will take a lot of time. Let's explore the most efficient card sorting algorithm with us.

Here is a trick, insert a stick in the hole on top of the cards, pull the cards up and bring them in front. Repeat the same procedure for rest of the holes and you will see all the cards are surprisingly sorted. Similarily, we can sort Ramayana cards of CCL IITGN based on the life of Lord Rama.
