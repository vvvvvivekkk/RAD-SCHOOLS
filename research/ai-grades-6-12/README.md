# Research: AI for Grades 6–12

Research pack for the Telangana private-school AI programme (Siddipet and
Hyderabad first; mostly CBSE, some State Board).

**Scope:** research only. No session plans, slides or modules in this folder.

**Status as of 21 November 2026:** scaffold in place, research NOT STARTED,
awaiting go-ahead. Only `00-plan.md` has real content.

## How to read this

Read `00-plan.md` first. It carries the research plan, what is being reused
from elsewhere in the repo, and five corrections to the original brief —
including two blockers that change what is deliverable.

## Conventions

- Every claim carries a link, and a label: **FACT / ESTIMATE / ASSUMPTION /
  OPINION / RECOMMENDATION**.
- India-specific findings are marked separately from global ones.
- The main outcome is **learning without the AI tool**. With-tool performance
  is reported separately and treated as secondary.
- A page that will not load is logged in `sources/failed-downloads.md`. A
  summary is never cited in place of the primary source.
- Any technical word is defined the first time it appears.

## Relationship to the rest of the repo

Large parts of the primary-source archive this brief asks for **already exist**
in this repo, on feature branches rather than on `main`. This folder points to
them rather than duplicating them. See `00-plan.md` §2.
