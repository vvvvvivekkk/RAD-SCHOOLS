# Legal / archival notes

## Fair-use + archival-mirror statement

This repository is a private offline archive built for curriculum
research for a Telangana K-12 education venture (Grades 5-10).

Content from third parties — CBSE, NCERT, SCERT Telangana, CISCE,
UNESCO, MIT, Raspberry Pi Foundation, Google DeepMind, DJI, Marut
Drones, Arduino, etc. — is kept here unmodified, with source URLs and
SHA-256 preserved in `download-manifest.md`, for private study,
curriculum development, and academic reference under the fair-use/fair-
dealing provisions of the Indian Copyright Act 1957 (s. 52) and the
US Copyright Act (17 USC §107).

## Policy

- No files are republished outside this private repo.
- Nothing here is passed off as our own work.
- Every third-party file lives alongside a per-folder README that
  cites the original publisher, source URL, and fetch date.
- Government-gazette PDFs (DGCA Drone Rules 2021, NEP 2020, NCERT
  materials) are reproduced in full as they are public records.
- Press articles are saved as evidence for citation durability only;
  the master pack lists each with `[press]` type.
- Unofficial textbook mirrors (teachersbadi.in, aglasem, selfstudys)
  are marked as mirrors in the manifest, with the original state
  SCERT URL noted where the source is a mirror.

## Removal requests

If you are a rights holder and want any file removed, open an issue on
https://github.com/vvvvvivekkk/RAD-SCHOOLS and it will be taken down.
