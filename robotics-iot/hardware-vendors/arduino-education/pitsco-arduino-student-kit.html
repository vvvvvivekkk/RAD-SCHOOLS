    Learn Electronics and Coding at Your Own Pace

    One complete Arduino workstation with twenty-five hours of guided curriculum teaches students ages eleven and up the fundamentals of circuits, coding, and digital logic—no prior experience needed.

    Self-Paced Curriculum

    Twenty-five hours of step-by-step lessons let each student progress at their own speed through circuits and coding.

    Complete Workstation

    Arduino UNO Rev3, breadboard, multimeter, and sensor components provide everything one student needs.

    Beginner-Friendly Design

    Created for children ages eleven and up with no prior electronics or coding experience.

    Confidence-Building Progression

    Students start with simple LED circuits and advance to temperature sensing and servo control.

    What Students Can Do

    From their very first session, students are building, coding, and problem-solving—developing real-world skills with every project.
    • Build circuits using a breadboard and electronic components,
    • Measure voltage and current with the included multimeter
    • Write Arduino code to control LEDs and read sensors
    • Program a servo motor for positional movement
    • Debug circuits and code through systematic troubleshooting
    • Apply Ohm's law and basic circuit theory

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What grade levels is the Arduino Education Student Kit designed for?

    The Arduino Education Student Kit is designed for 6–12.

    Do students need prior experience?

    No prior coding or electronics experience is required. The included materials guide students step by step from the very beginning.

    What software is needed?

    Students use the free Arduino Software (IDE), which is available for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Some products also support the Arduino Cloud platform.

    How many students can use this product?

    The Arduino Education Student Kit serves 1.

    Is this compatible with other Arduino products?

    Yes. Arduino products are designed to work together. Students can expand their learning by adding sensor kits, shields, and additional boards.
